Actuator study

The animation follows the architecture in Benjamin Katz (2018), Figure 2-2: a motor, planetary transmission, bearings, and controller within one module. The thesis figure itself is not reproduced.

Mechanical geometry
Mini-Cheetah-Actuator by Saketh vegesna (SakethGG), using the published STEP parts. MIT License.
Controller geometry
moteus C1 by mjbots, via a public GoForFab KiCad export. Apache License 2.0. Component models follow the KiCad library license and generated-design exception.

This is a study using MIT-licensed Mini-Cheetah replica CAD and a representative moteus C1 controller. It is not an exact reconstruction of Katz’s hardware or electronics, or a dimensionally validated assembly for fabrication.

The source parts were converted from STEP, aligned, separated for the exploded view, and given steel-grey presentation materials. The live animation uses a compressed display mesh produced with glTF-Transform and meshoptimizer; all 69 mechanical and controller parts remain, and the original model is retained. Asset provenance and full attribution record the original files, revisions, and conversion details.

Graph methodology

The graph uses source-linked observations, rebased to each measure’s observed 2020 value: normalized value = 100 × raw value ÷ that measure’s 2020 value. Every plotted measure therefore equals 100 in 2020. The normalization compares change within each measure; equal heights do not mean equal capability, scale, or economic value across measures.

Points are published observations or documented annual averages. Connecting segments are visual guides, not additional observations. Missing years remain missing. No values are extrapolated into the future, and the former illustrative curves and scenario bands are not used.

LLM performance

Selected MMLU 5-shot scores. Five reported model results on the original English benchmark, from GPT-3 in 2020 to Claude 3.5 Sonnet in 2024. Baseline: 43.9% accuracy. These are selected results, not annual averages or a comprehensive frontier. Providers and evaluation implementations differ; only standard 5-shot scores are included. Twice the baseline accuracy does not mean twice the intelligence.

MMLU; Gopher; PaLM; GPT-4; Claude 3.5 Sonnet (benchmark table).

Robot prices

Bank of Japan Robots producer-price index. Series PRCG20_1201350029, excluding consumption tax. Each point is the arithmetic mean of twelve published monthly observations for a complete calendar year. Baseline: 100.008333… PPI, the 2020 mean of the rounded monthly data. That mean is rebased to exactly 100 in this graph.

The series tracks domestically produced and traded whole robots in Japan, with the BOJ’s quality adjustments. It is not USD per actuator or a global affordability measure. Higher values mean higher producer prices. No currency conversion, inversion, or general-inflation adjustment is applied. Incomplete 2026 data is excluded.

BOJ monthly data (ZIP); download directory; 2020-base methodology.

No comparable annual series verified. No numerical curve or baseline is assigned. HumanoidBench supplies a simulated whole-body control benchmark, not a comparable annual history of general humanoid capability. Demonstration videos remain research context.

Interaction data

No comparable annual series verified. No numerical curve or baseline is assigned. Open X-Embodiment and DROID describe different corpora, tasks, collection methods, and episode lengths. Nested collections are not summed, and hours are not combined with trajectory counts. Teleoperation, UMI, and simulation stories remain research context.
