Actuator study

The animation follows the architecture in Benjamin Katz (2018), Figure 2-2: a motor, planetary transmission, bearings, and controller within one module. The thesis figure itself is not reproduced.

Mechanical geometry
Mini-Cheetah-Actuator by Saketh vegesna (SakethGG), using the published STEP parts. MIT License.
Controller geometry
moteus C1 by mjbots, via a public GoForFab KiCad export. Apache License 2.0. Component models follow the KiCad library license and generated-design exception.

This is a study using MIT-licensed Mini-Cheetah replica CAD and a representative moteus C1 controller. It is not an exact reconstruction of Katz’s hardware or electronics, or a dimensionally validated assembly for fabrication.

The source parts were converted from STEP, aligned, separated for the exploded view, and given steel-grey presentation materials. Mechanical geometry comes from the credited CAD. Asset provenance and full attribution record the original files, revisions, and conversion details.

Graph methodology

The trajectories are synthetic design-study curves on a normalized 0–100 index. They are not measured robotics data or forecasts. The curve values and the 2026 divider demonstrate the visual comparison and interaction.

The existing curve shapes are preserved in the graph definitions. Labels such as LLM performance, deployment, and hardware affordability describe concepts; the shared index does not establish a quantitative equivalence between them.
