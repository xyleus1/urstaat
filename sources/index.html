Graph sources

LLM performance
METR task-completion horizons — human task duration associated with 80% model success. Published results.
Humanoid deployment
Counterpoint Research, 2025 installations and 2026 shipments. Shipments provide the deployment proxy.
Robot deployment and specialist robots
IFR World Robotics 2026 and historical charts — annual industrial robot installations.
Interaction data
Released training episodes from RoboNet, DROID, and AgiBot World. AgiBot Beta replaces its Alpha subset.
Hardware cost
Bank of Japan producer-price index, Robots, excluding consumption tax — series PRCG20_1201350029. Lower values mean lower prices.

Historical trajectories combine published observations with interpolation and backward extensions. Display values use one shared compression, with 2025 set to 100 and the ceiling set to 10,000. Source measurements and curve shapes remain unchanged; the compressed values show relative trajectories rather than literal growth multiples.

Dashed lines are authored scenarios. Their growth, timing, and plateaus are assumptions, informed by AI 2040’s takeoff forecast and scaling strategy. The humanoid and total-deployment curves follow the modeled LLM trajectory; they are not publisher forecasts.
