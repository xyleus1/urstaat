Architecture reference: Benjamin Katz (2018), Figure 2-2, PDF page 26.
Motor, planetary transmission, bearings, and controller within a single actuator module.
https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/b85069e2-f1cd-470e-a92a-9bf0dadfa7ee
The thesis figure is not reproduced in the website. This is a sourced mechanical
study using independently published CAD and a representative controller, not an
exact reconstruction of Katz's original hardware or controller.

Changes: STEP tessellation through CadQuery/OpenCascade; rigid alignment and
translations for the exploded presentation; the controller's material primitives
merged without changing their geometry; presentation materials changed to steel
greys. The visualization uses 69 meshes and 973,838 source triangles. No original
mechanical shapes were authored. The assembled mix is not a dimensionally
validated or buildable actuator design.
